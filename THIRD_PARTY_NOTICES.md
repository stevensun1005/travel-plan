# Third-party licenses

- Leaflet 1.9.4, BSD-2-Clause. Distribution and original license are included in `assets/starter/vendor/leaflet/`. The visible Leaflet attribution is retained.
- © OpenStreetMap contributors. Map data is provided under ODbL; tiles load on demand from OpenStreetMap. The map visibly links to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. No bulk tile download is bundled.
- Offline base maps (`basemap.json`, including `assets/demo-basemap.json`) are simplified extracts of OpenStreetMap data retrieved through the Overpass API, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. They are drawn under the tiles with the same visible OSM attribution.
- World outline (`vendor/world/countries-110m.json` in the starter and demo) is simplified from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries, which is in the public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). It keeps country outlines, label points and names only; boundaries are approximate and not authoritative.
- Handwriting fonts Patrick Hand and Ma Shan Zheng (SIL Open Font License) are loaded on demand from Google Fonts; no font files are bundled. Local fallbacks are used when they are unavailable.
- The demo road cache was retrieved from the OSRM public service using OpenStreetMap data. It contains road estimates, not traffic predictions. Provider URLs and generation date are in `assets/demo-routes.json`.
- Retained travel-template code conventions and portions are covered by the MIT notice in `licenses/travel-template-MIT.txt`. The license is preserved; no upstream geographic boundary libraries or frozen schematic maps are included.

The Vancouver starter demo's hotels, airfares and prices are fictional. Its landmark coordinates are public map reference points, not representations of private accommodation or confirmed transport entrances.
